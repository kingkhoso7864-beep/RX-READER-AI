## 📌 About the Project

**Rx Reader AI** is an AI-powered healthcare assistance application designed to help users understand, organize, and manage information from medical prescriptions.

Patients may face difficulties reading handwritten prescriptions, remembering medication timings, managing multiple medicines, and keeping track of previous prescriptions.

Rx Reader AI brings these tasks together in one simple digital experience using:

**AI + Prescription Processing + Medication Scheduling + Notifications + Safety Assistance + Prescription History**

The goal is to make prescription and medication management more organized, accessible, and user-friendly.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Managing medical prescriptions can be challenging.

Users may experience:

- Difficult-to-read handwritten prescriptions
- Forgotten medication timings
- Difficulty managing multiple medicines
- Lost or disorganized prescription records
- Difficulty understanding medication information
- Lack of an organized medication routine
