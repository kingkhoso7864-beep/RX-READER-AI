# 📌 About Rx Reader AI

**Rx Reader AI** is an AI-powered application designed to help users understand, organize, and manage information from medical prescriptions.

Medical prescriptions can sometimes be difficult to read, especially when handwritten. Patients may also forget medication timings, lose previous prescriptions, or struggle to manage multiple medicines.

Rx Reader AI aims to simplify this experience by bringing **AI-powered prescription processing, medication scheduling, reminders, prescription history, safety assistance, and an AI assistant** together in one application.

The goal is simple:

> **Transform a difficult-to-manage prescription into a smarter and more organized digital health experience.**

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Patients and caregivers may face several everyday challenges:

- Difficult-to-read handwritten prescriptions
- Forgetting medication timings
- Managing multiple medicines
- Losing or misplacing previous prescriptions
- Difficulty organizing medication information
- Limited access to understandable medication information
- Difficulty keeping track of daily medication routines

These challenges inspired the development of Rx Reader AI.
